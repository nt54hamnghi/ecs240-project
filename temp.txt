Function-related grammar rules (restored in proposal.tex; retained for reference)

<func-stmt>      ::= "fun" IDENT "(" <param>? ")" <block>
<param>          ::= IDENT ("," IDENT)*
<return-stmt>    ::= "return" <expr>? ";"
<call-expr>      ::= IDENT "(" <args>? ")"
<args>           ::= <expr> ("," <expr>)*

Restored alternatives:
- <stmt>: <func-stmt> | <return-stmt>
- <primary>: <call-expr>

Restored restriction note: return is only allowed inside functions, and
nested function definitions are disallowed.
